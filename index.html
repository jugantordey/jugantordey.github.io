About

Where I come from and what I am working on.

I am from Dhaka. I went to Ideal School and College in Motijheel, then to Notre Dame College, and I finished my HSC in 2026. I am on a gap year now, and most of it goes to physics.

I started teaching myself university physics in class 10, outside the national curriculum. About 2,500 hours later I have finished both volumes of Halliday, Resnick and Krane and solved almost every problem in the first. Mechanics is my strongest area.

I also write, teach and design for the projects above, and I help raise my twin siblings, who are nine years younger. There is more on the Beyond physics page.
