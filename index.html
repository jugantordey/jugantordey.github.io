Handout series · Bangla · Free

Mathematics for the Physics Olympiad

A 22-chapter series that takes a Bangla-medium student from class-5 arithmetic to vector calculus. It covers the calculus the physics olympiad needs and no more, and assumes nothing beyond arithmetic, LCM and GCD, fractions and decimals. Every chapter comes as a student edition and as an edition with worked solutions.

About

I went to Ideal School and College in Dhaka until the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) examination, then to Notre Dame College, where I sat the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) examination in 2026.

I represented Bangladesh at the Asian Physics Olympiad 2025 in Dhahran, Saudi Arabia, where I earned an Honorable Mention. I was on the national team for the Asian Physics Olympiad 2026 in Busan, South Korea.

I served as Executive of Academics at the Notre Dame Math Club (2025–26), where I co-edited The Set, a series of olympiad mathematics articles written by club members. Only the first volume has been published so far.
