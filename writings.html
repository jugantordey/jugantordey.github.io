Books

The one I edit.

The Set is a Bangla math olympiad book series from the Notre Dame Math Club.

Volume I has 20 articles and 280 pages, typeset in LaTeX, and came out in March 2026. The full series will have about 90 articles by more than 30 authors across five volumes.

I am one of four student editors. I translated the English articles into Bangla, wrote the editorials, and organized the cover unveiling at the Amar Ekushey Book Fair. We sold 190 copies of Volume I without paid ads.
