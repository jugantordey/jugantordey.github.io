Mathematics for the Physics Olympiad

Bangladeshi students have almost nothing serious or organised to read in their own language, sitting between school textbooks and university-level texts in English. That gap does more damage to physics here than any shortage of talent. Curiosity is not what's missing; what stops students from going deeper is the mathematics physics runs on, calculus above all. That was my own situation. Coming from the Bangla-medium curriculum, I found the start rough, and the English material harder going than the physics inside it. I doubt much has changed for anyone with the same background. These 22 chapters, written in Bengali, are my attempt at removing two obstacles at once: the language, and the absence of anyone to guide you through. They run from class-5 arithmetic to vector calculus, cover the calculus the physics olympiad actually needs and no more, and assume nothing beyond arithmetic, LCM and GCD, fractions and decimals.

No teacher is required; read the chapters in order. Each chapter title below links to the student edition, and (Sol) to the same chapter with worked solutions to every problem. Try the problems first, and stay stuck for at least half an hour before opening a solution. The number beside each problem, from [1] to [5], is its difficulty.
