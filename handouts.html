Mathematics for the Physics Olympiad

Students in Bangladesh have little to read in Bangla between school textbooks and university texts in English. The math that physics needs, calculus above all, is where many of them get stuck. I got stuck there too.

These 22 chapters are written in Bangla for a student who has no teacher for this. They start at class-5 arithmetic, end at vector calculus, and cover the calculus the physics olympiad needs and no more.

Read the chapters in order, try the problems first, and stay stuck for at least half an hour before you open a solution.
